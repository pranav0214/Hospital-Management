🌍 Gulf Region — UAE · Dubai · Abu Dhabi · Qatar · Saudi Arabia
1 key platform
!

Gulf compliance is non-negotiable. Any HMS operating in the UAE must be connected to NABIDH (Dubai Health Authority), Riayati (Abu Dhabi), or NPHIES (Saudi Arabia). These are government-mandated health data networks — not optional integrations. This is the biggest barrier to entry in this market.
